Privacy Notice

Application: Finance Tracker Local

Finance Tracker Local is a private, non-commercial application developed for the account owner's personal financial tracking. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Data accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access account balances, account identifiers, transaction dates, amounts, currencies, and transaction descriptions through Enable Banking's account information service.

Purpose and storage

The information is used solely to track, categorize, and summarize the owner's personal finances. The application's own processing and storage are intended to take place in the owner's private environment.

Third-party connection

The bank connection is provided through Enable Banking and the account-servicing bank. Their processing of information is subject to their respective notices and applicable terms. The application does not initiate payments.

Access and control

Only the account owner is intended to use the application. The owner can revoke the bank connection through the applicable bank or connection provider's controls.

Contact

Data protection contact details are supplied to Enable Banking during application registration.
