Terms of Use

Application: Finance Tracker Local

Finance Tracker Local is a private, experimental, non-commercial application intended solely for the owner's personal use. It is not offered to the public as a financial service.

Functionality

The application may retrieve authorized account information and transaction history using Enable Banking, then organize and present that information for personal budgeting and expense tracking.

Read-only access

The intended integration is read-only. It does not initiate payments, transfer money, or provide banking services.

Accuracy

Reports, categorization, and summaries may contain errors. Official bank statements and records remain authoritative.

Access

Access to account information requires the owner's authorization and may be revoked through the relevant bank or connection provider.
